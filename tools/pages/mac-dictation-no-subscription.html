Processing and billing are different choices

A plan may cover recognition, online services or editing tools. Check what is included rather than inferring processing location from the billing model.

Aria’s Automatic mode, offered at first launch, uses the online service supplied during the beta without a key and with limits. To process speech on your Mac, download a model such as NVIDIA Parakeet TDT V3 and choose Local.

What "free" gets you

Aria Free includes 2,000 words a week, reset every Monday. In Local mode, audio and transcription are processed on your Mac; quota or licence checks may need a connection. In Automatic mode, audio is sent to the online service.

The current beta does not offer a Pro purchase or an unlimited plan. Evaluate Aria using the allowance that is actually available.

And what about Apple's built-in dictation?

Apple Dictation is free and already included with macOS. Start there and try your usual vocabulary. If you need file transcription, a searchable history or a choice of engine, compare dedicated apps on those features.

Questions

Can I buy Aria Pro?

Not during the current beta. The available offer is 2,000 free words a week.

Is there a catch in the free tier?

The limit is 2,000 words per week, checked against a server counter so the quota cannot be reset by reinstalling. Local dictation itself sends nothing: no audio, no transcript.

Do I need to pay a cloud provider separately?

The Groq service supplied by Aria during the beta needs no key. Beta conditions and quotas still apply. A downloaded local model processes speech on your Mac.

What if I have several Macs?

Separate Apple Silicon and Intel downloads are available. Buying a multi-Mac Pro licence is not open during the beta.
