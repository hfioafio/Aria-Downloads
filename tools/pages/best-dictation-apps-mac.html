The question that actually decides it

Not "which is best". These apps split cleanly along two lines.

Do you dictate, or do you transcribe? Dictating means talking and having text appear where you are working — Aria, Wispr Flow, Superwhisper and VoiceInk support that; MacWhisper also includes dictation in its website-distributed edition. Transcribing means feeding in a recording — MacWhisper has extensive file tools and also supports dictation in its website-distributed edition. Aria does both, with the emphasis on dictation.

Check the selected mode and each app’s policy. In Aria, Local mode processes speech on your Mac after a model download; Automatic mode, offered at first launch, uses an online service. Quota and licence checks may need a connection even in Local mode.

A subscription is not unreasonable when the vendor pays a cloud model per minute — that is genuinely their cost. It is worth knowing which side of that line you are buying on.

Where Aria loses

  • macOS only. No Windows, no iPhone. If you switch machines during the day, Superwhisper or Wispr Flow fit better.
  • Not the file specialist. For hours of audio, batches, and subtitle work, MacWhisper is the more focused tool.
  • Text transformations. Compare modes using your own texts and check the terms of the service used.
  • No enterprise paperwork. No SSO, no SOC 2, no HIPAA. That is Wispr Flow's territory.
  • Not notarised by Apple yet. The first launch needs System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. It is one step, once, and it is a real annoyance.
  • Small and new. One developer, no track record, no reviews yet. That is a legitimate reason to wait.

Where Aria wins

On a Mac, Aria combines dictation at the cursor, a personal dictionary, file transcription and local history. Download a model and choose Local to process speech on your Mac; quota or licence checks may still need a connection.

Questions

What is the best free dictation app for Mac?

Apple Dictation if you dictate rarely — it is built in and offline. For daily use with correct punctuation, a personal dictionary and file transcription, Aria is free up to 2,000 words a week, and MacWhisper has a free version aimed at files.

What should I check for local dictation?

Check the model, languages and app’s network requirements. Aria offers local models on Apple Silicon and Intel; they do not upload audio for recognition, but quota and licence checks may require a connection.
