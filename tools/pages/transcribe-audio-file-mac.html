Use case

Transcribe an audio file on your Mac, without uploading it

Import an audio or video file to get a transcript. To process it on your Mac, download a model and choose Local. Online mode sends audio to the transcription service; check your selected mode before starting.

What you can drop in

AudioMP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG
VideoMP4, MOV — the soundtrack is transcribed
Speaker labelsOptional, computed on the Mac
Languages25 European languages, and the transcript keeps the language spoken
NetworkNo audio upload in Local mode; quota/licence checks may use the network

How it goes

  1. Open Aria and go to file transcription.
  2. Drop the file in. Long recordings work; they simply take longer.
  3. Read, correct, copy or export. Words you fix are learned, to help guide later recognition of recurring names.

Why do it on the machine

Online transcription requires sending audio to a service. Its retention policy depends on the provider and plan. For a confidential recording, check those conditions before uploading, or choose a local engine.

In Local mode, the file is transcribed on your Mac without uploading audio for recognition. Processing time depends on the model, duration and Mac; transcribed words count towards the free allowance.

Getting a better transcript

  • The recording matters more than the model. A phone on a table in a café will defeat any transcription tool. A microphone near each speaker changes everything.
  • Teach it the names first. Adding recurring proper nouns to the dictionary before a long file saves more editing than any setting.
  • Split very long files if you want to work through them in passes rather than waiting for the whole thing.
  • Compare a sample. The online mode supplied during the beta is another option, without a key and with limits. It uploads audio for transcription.

What the exports provide. Aria exports TXT, SRT and VTT. Subtitles use the transcript’s timestamps; review the text and segment timing before video editing. Aria does not join a meeting on your behalf.

Questions

Is anything uploaded?

Not with the local engine. The file is read from disk and transcribed on the Mac. A cloud engine only sees the file if you deliberately pick one.

How long can a recording be?

Long files are processed in segments; duration and available resources affect processing time. Recognised words count towards the 2,000 free words a week. Pro purchases are closed during the beta, so do not rely on an unlimited upgrade to finish a long file.

Does it separate speakers?

Yes, locally. It is good on a clean two- or three-person recording and degrades on a noisy room with many voices; expect to fix a few labels.

Can I export subtitles?

Yes, TXT, SRT and VTT exports are available. Review recognition and timestamps before publishing.
