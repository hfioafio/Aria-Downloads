Compare the offer you can actually use

Compare the free allowance, paid-plan terms and the features you need. An old monthly price or a projected cost over several years cannot replace checking the current terms.

Aria’s free allowance lets you test dictation in your own apps. It is not unlimited: the limit is 2,000 words a week across dictation and transcription.

Choose where speech is processed

Processing location matters when you dictate confidential information. Check each service’s privacy policy and network requirements rather than inferring its behaviour from the product name.

Aria lets you download a model such as NVIDIA Parakeet TDT V3, then choose Local. That mode processes audio and transcription on your Mac. Automatic mode, offered at first launch, uses the online service supplied by Aria during the beta, without a key and with limits.

What “local” means here. The model and history stay on your Mac. Local recognition does not upload audio for transcription; quota or licence checks may still need a connection.

It writes what you said

Aria’s faithful formatting keeps the recognised words. Transformations such as Email and Key points are optional. Language detection, names and language switches can be misrecognised: review the result and choose an explicit language when needed.

The personal dictionary retains corrections to names and vocabulary to guide later recognition. It helps the engine without guaranteeing a term will always be recognised correctly.

Where Wispr Flow is the better choice

This page would be worthless if it pretended Aria wins everything. Pick Wispr Flow if:

  • You are not only on a Mac. Wispr Flow runs on Windows, iOS and Android. Aria is macOS only, and there is no phone app.
  • You need compliance paperwork. SSO, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA — Aria has none of that, and will not pretend otherwise.
  • You need a service for a team. Compare support and administration terms; Aria is an independent app in beta.
  • You want a fully notarised installer today. Aria is signed with a stable identity but not yet notarised by Apple, so the first launch needs one trip through System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Try it on a first message

  1. Download the DMG for your Mac and drag Aria into Applications.
  2. Open it once; if macOS blocks it, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Grant Microphone and Accessibility — Accessibility is what lets Aria type into other apps.
  4. For local processing, download a model and choose Local; then use the shortcut shown in Aria to dictate.

Questions people actually ask

Is Aria really usable with no internet at all?

A downloaded local model produces text on your Mac. Quota or licence checks may need internet; this does not guarantee fully disconnected use.

Can I buy Aria Pro?

Pro purchases are closed during the current beta. The available offer is 2,000 free words a week.

Can I use an online mode?

Yes. Automatic mode uses the Groq service supplied by Aria during the beta, without a key and with limits. Audio is then sent for online transcription.

Does Aria work in Slack, Notion, VS Code, a browser?

Yes. Aria types at the cursor, so any standard macOS text field works — Mail, Messages, Notes, Slack, Teams, browsers, editors.

Why does macOS warn me on first launch?

Aria is signed with a stable identity but not yet notarised by Apple. Try to open it once, then use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Only do that for a file from this site or the official GitHub repository.
