Compare workflows before prices

Both apps put dictation behind a keyboard shortcut. Try recognition, model selection and text transformations on a few messages representative of your work.

Aria’s free allowance is 2,000 words a week for dictation and transcription. You can try a downloaded local model or the beta’s online service to see whether the workflow fits.

Parakeet, not only Whisper

Aria’s recommended local engine is NVIDIA Parakeet TDT V3. Whisper is also available locally. Results depend on the language, microphone and recording; another engine may suit a particular case better.

Faithful by default

Aria writes what you said. It adds capitals and full stops locally, and it does not summarise, translate or restyle unless you pick one of those modes explicitly. If you like heavily prompt-driven rewriting, that is a genuine reason to prefer Superwhisper — see below.

Where Superwhisper is the better choice

  • Transformations. Compare Superwhisper’s modes with Aria’s optional text transformations using your own examples.
  • Windows and iPhone. Aria is macOS only.
  • A notarised, fully mature installer. Aria is signed but not yet notarised, so the first launch needs System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • Model choice. Check the models available and their terms in each app.

Questions

Does Aria work with no internet?

After downloading a model, Local mode processes audio and produces text on your Mac. Quota or licence checks may need internet; this is not a guarantee of fully disconnected use.

Can I buy Aria Pro?

Pro purchases are closed during the current beta. The available offer includes 2,000 free words a week.

Can I transcribe existing audio files?

Yes — MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, MP4, MOV — including in the free version.
