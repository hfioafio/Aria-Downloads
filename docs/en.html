Privacy you can actually read

No small print hidden behind “AI”.

The mode you pick states plainly what stays on the Mac and what reaches your cloud provider.

01Local means local

With Parakeet, neither the audio nor the transcript leaves your computer.

02No server key shipped in the app

The bundled Groq mode uses a key held on the server. Your own optional keys stay encrypted in the macOS Keychain.

03Adjustable history

Keep it, cap it or switch it off, depending on how private you need to be.

04Optional screen context

Local screen reading for proper nouns can be disabled at any time.

05Optional measurement

Pseudonymous usage statistics are off by default. If you turn them on, only counters and a random identifier are shared: never audio, text, email, name or hardware identifier. Opting out deletes the related aggregates. Frequently asked questions.

Does Aria work offline?

Yes. With the local engine the audio stays on the Mac and nothing is sent anywhere. Aria Free still needs a brief connection to check the weekly word counter.

Does Aria translate my speech into English?

No. The transcript keeps the language you spoke. Transformations such as Email or Key points only apply when you choose them.

Which Mac apps does it work in?

Aria types at the cursor, so it works in Mail, Messages, Notes, Slack, Teams, browsers, code editors and any standard macOS text field.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not with the local mode. For now the Groq mode can run with no key to enter; the audio then travels through a rate-limited Aria relay to Groq, with no storage of audio or text. A personal key remains possible for the other optional services.

How does the free limit work?

Aria Free includes 2,000 words per week and resets every Monday on server time. A brief connection is needed to check the counter; reinstalling the app or changing the Mac clock does not create a new quota.

Is the €5 a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase giving lifetime access to Aria Pro on three Macs, with unlimited words and all future updates included.

What happens during a long dictation?

The cloud timeout grows with the recorded duration. Two minutes of speech gets a far longer allowance than a short sentence before the local fallback is even considered.

Does Aria work with AirPods?

Yes. Aria can follow the microphone chosen in macOS, or use a device you select explicitly in its settings.

Why does macOS show a warning?

Aria is signed but not yet notarised by Apple. After a first open attempt, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The upcoming Developer ID build removes this step.

How do updates work?

Aria checks the public manifest at launch and then every six hours. It resumes the download of the DMG matching your Mac when needed, checks its size, its SHA-256 and its Apple identity, then updates the app in place. If the new version fails to start, Aria restores the previous one automatically.
