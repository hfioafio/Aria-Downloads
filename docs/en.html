What it actually does

  • Inserts text in the frontmost app, then gets out of the way.
  • Local mode keeps audio and transcription on the Mac. During the beta, Groq online mode also works without entering a key through a limited Aria relay that stores neither audio nor text.
  • Free: 2,000 words a week, reset every Monday. Pro is €5 once, three Macs, no subscription.
  • Updates are checked, hashed, and rolled back if the new build fails to start.
