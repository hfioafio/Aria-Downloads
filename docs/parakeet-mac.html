Why Parakeet rather than Whisper

Parakeet is Aria’s recommended local engine; Whisper remains available. Try both on your phrases and recordings: quality and latency depend on the language, microphone, model and Mac.

Automatic mode uses the online service supplied by Aria during the beta, without a key and with limits. Local processing requires explicitly choosing Local after downloading a model.

The part that is usually the hard bit

Running a local ASR model is the easy half. The hard half is everything around it, and that is what an app is for:

  • Getting the text into the app you are in. Aria types at the cursor in Mail, Slack, Notes, a browser, an editor, so there is no window to copy out of.
  • Not losing the first or last word. Recording starts before the meter appears on screen, and the tail of the sentence is captured rather than cut at the moment you release the key.
  • Microphone changes. AirPods that disconnect mid-sentence fall back to the built-in mic without interrupting the dictation.
  • Vocabulary. Correct a name once and it is added to a personal dictionary, to guide later recognition.
  • A model for your Mac. Local processing uses the machine’s resources; consumption depends on the model and recording length.

What it costs you in machine terms

The model occupies disk space and uses memory while running. Consumption and speed vary with the Mac and its current workload. Aria provides separate native builds for Apple Silicon and Intel.

Compare engines on your audio. An accent, noise or unusual names can change the result. Try a representative sample; online Automatic mode is another option, without a guarantee of better accuracy on every recording.

Questions

Do I need to install Python, conda or anything else?

No. The runtime is bundled in the app. Install the DMG, download the model from inside Aria, and dictate.

What does local processing mean?

After downloading a model and choosing Local, audio and transcription are processed on your Mac. Quota or licence checks may require internet; fully disconnected use is not guaranteed.

Which languages does Parakeet TDT V3 cover?

25 European languages. Choose an explicit language if automatic detection is wrong; language switches may need review.

Can I use Whisper instead?

Yes, Whisper is also available locally. For online processing, Automatic mode uses the service supplied by Aria during the beta, without a key and with limits.

Does it run on an Intel Mac?

Yes. There is a separate Intel build. It is slower than on Apple Silicon but it works.
