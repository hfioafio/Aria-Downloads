Why a subscription is the norm — and why Aria skips it

Most of these apps send your audio to a cloud model and pay per minute for it. A recurring fee is the honest way to cover a recurring cost. That logic is real, and it is the reason Wispr Flow and Superwhisper charge monthly.

Aria's default engine is different: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT V3, running on your Mac. Transcribing on hardware you already own costs the developer nothing per word, so there is nothing to bill monthly. Cloud engines stay available — OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, Soniox, ElevenLabs, Mistral — but with your own API key, so the provider bills you directly at their price, with no margin added.

What "free" gets you

Aria Free is 2,000 words a week, reset every Monday, with both local and cloud engines available. That is roughly four to six emails a day — enough to know within a week whether the app belongs in your workflow. The counter is checked on a server so reinstalling does not hand you a fresh quota; nothing else about your dictation is sent anywhere.

Aria Pro is €5, one time, for unlimited words on up to three Macs, with all future updates included. There is no seat count, no renewal, and no upgrade fee for the next major version.

And what about Apple's built-in dictation?

It is free, it is already installed, and for short bursts it is fine — start there before paying anyone anything. Where people usually leave it: punctuation and formatting, proper nouns and jargon it will never learn, no transcription of existing audio files, no choice of engine, and no way to see or search what you dictated last week. If none of that bothers you, macOS already has you covered.

Questions

What exactly do I get for €5?

Unlimited words, a perpetual licence on three Macs, and every future update. No renewal and no separate charge for major versions.

Is there a catch in the free tier?

The limit is 2,000 words per week, checked against a server counter so the quota cannot be reset by reinstalling. Local dictation itself sends nothing: no audio, no transcript.

Do I have to pay a cloud provider on top?

Not with the local engine, and not with the bundled Groq mode. If you want another cloud engine, you use your own API key and the provider bills you directly.

What is the price if I have two Macs?

Still €5. The licence covers three Macs.
